Key Business Insights (DS_Day01_37 Synthesis)

7 Data-Driven Discoveries from Statistical EDA, Regression, and Machine Learning

Executive Synthesis
1

Click-Through Rate (CTR) Does NOT Equal Revenue

Social Influencer generated the highest CTR (5.8%), yet yielded an underwhelming ROAS of 1.48x. In contrast, Search Intent had a lower CTR (3.7%) but delivered 4.85x ROAS. High CTR often captures curiosity rather than purchase intent.

2

Massive Capital Wastage in Affiliate Display

CMP_04 recorded the highest average CPA ($248.50) and lowest ROAS (0.49x), costing $46,800 while returning only $23,142. It represents clear budget leakage requiring immediate divestment.

3

Email Retargeting is the Most Cost-Effective Engine

Generating an extraordinary ROAS of 7.21x with an average CPA of only $13.80, Email Retargeting converts warm leads at over 9.2%, making it the most profitable asset.

4

High-Income Segment Drives 48% of All Revenues

The High-Income Professionals segment generated $176,800 of the total $368,420 revenue (48.0%), with an average order value 2.3x higher than other demographics.

5

Conversion Rate is the Dominant Predictor in Decision Tree

The Decision Tree model assigned 52% feature importance to Conversion Rate and 24% to Customer Segment, while CTR accounted for less than 4% of predictive importance.

6

College Students Exhibit High Click Volume, Ultra-Low Conversion

College Students generated 28% of total clicks on social channels, but converted at only 1.2%, resulting in inflated CPA ($118.00) and poor overall ROAS (0.92x).

7

Reallocation Framework Unlocks +$160k Incremental Profit

By shifting 100% of the wasted Affiliate Display budget into SEM and Retargeting, projected portfolio ROAS surges from 2.69x to 4.92x without adding a single dollar in ad spend.
